## About

### The problem and the idea

In an obstetric emergency, new staff must report fast and clearly. It is hard to practice that under real pressure.

- New midwives and nurses must call a doctor in an emergency
- They need to say the most dangerous fact first
- SBAR Call lets them practice this call by voice
- The scene is postpartum hemorrhage

### How it works

You call a doctor, speak your SBAR report, and see your results right away.

- Choose the postpartum hemorrhage scenario and read the patient chart
- Tap to call, then speak your report with voice recognition
- No microphone? Type your report in the backup text box
- Two meters, Priority and Brevity, plus feedback on missing info and filler words

### Why it matters and what's next

Short, clear reports help a doctor act quickly. Practice builds that habit before a real emergency.

- You can retry and see your recent scores
- The tone is soft pink and cream, with Japanese and English
- Starts with one scene so it can be finished well
- Next: CTG reading and reporting, already shown as Coming soon
